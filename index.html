The insight

Every contract you draft is basically the same contract.
The names change. The dates change. The words don't.

You already know this agreement clause by clause. What eats the time is retyping it, then chasing it through Google Docs for one client, Word for another, an email thread for whoever it lands on, and one more tool just to get it signed.

1

Upload once. Draft a hundred.

Three steps, no retyping. Everything happens in the browser, with nothing to install or configure.

Upload

Drop in a .docx, give it a name, and tag what kind of document it is: an NDA, a services agreement, an offer letter, whatever you draft most.

Mark

Select the parts that change from contract to contract, things like the client name, dates, and dollar amounts, and label them. The rest of the document, and all its formatting, stays untouched.

Draft

Pick the template, fill in a short form, and generate. You get a live preview and a real .docx, ready to send, saved automatically to your library.

2

Click into it. Edit it like a person, not a form.

Insert a sentence, fix a typo, swap a clause, right in the document itself. Stage as many changes as you want and watch every one of them land in place before you save anything.

Features

A few more things worth knowing.

Instant use

No training, no setup calls, nothing to install. Upload a .docx and your first template is ready in under a minute.

Self-running approvals

Set a rule once per field: a range, a list of approved values, or always flag. Redlines inside the rule are auto-accepted, so only the real asks need you.

Legally binding e-signatures

Every contract is signed through a dedicated e-signature service, with a timestamped record of who signed and when.

Built-in ID and selfie checks

Signers confirm who they are with a photo ID and a selfie before signing. No separate verification service to pay for.

Signing order and reminders

Choose who signs first. Each signer is emailed when it’s their turn, and anyone who’s waiting gets a nudge.

Comment threads on every redline

Talk through a change right next to it, with your client, until it’s resolved. No side email threads.
