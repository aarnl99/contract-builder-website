Upload once. Draft a hundred.

Three steps, no retyping. Everything happens in the browser, with nothing to install or configure.

Upload

Drop in a .docx, give it a name, and tag what kind of document it is: an NDA, a services agreement, an offer letter, whatever you draft most.

Mark

Select the parts that change from contract to contract, things like the client name, dates, and dollar amounts, and label them. The rest of the document, and all its formatting, stays untouched.

Draft

Pick the template, fill in a short form, and generate. You get a live preview, saved automatically to your library and ready to send for review or signature without leaving Rotely. Need a file? Export the .docx anytime.

Features

A few more things worth knowing.

Instant use

No training, no setup calls, nothing to install. Upload a .docx and your first template is ready in under a minute.

Self-running approvals

Set a rule once per field: a range, a list of approved values, or always flag. Redlines inside the rule are auto-accepted, so only the real asks need you.

Your formatting, untouched

Fonts, numbering, tables and signature blocks come through every draft exactly as they were in your original.

One library for every deal

Drafts, redline rounds and signed copies live together, so there’s never a question of which version is real.

Signing order and reminders

Choose who signs first. Each signer is emailed when it’s their turn, and anyone who’s waiting gets a nudge.

Comment threads on every redline

Talk through a change right next to it, with your client, until it’s resolved. No side email threads.

Security & privacy

Your contracts stay yours.

You’re trusting Rotely with signed agreements and signers’ IDs. Here’s how we treat them.

Private by default

Templates, drafts and signed contracts are visible only to your account and the people you choose to share them with.

Never used to train AI

Your documents are never used to train any AI model. AI only works on a document when you ask it to.

Signatures powered by DocuSeal

Every signature is a legally binding e-signature under the U.S. ESIGN Act and UETA, with a timestamped audit trail.

ID checks used only to verify

Photo ID and selfie checks are optional. When they’re on, the photos are used only to confirm who signed and stay with that contract’s signing record.

Secure share links

Clients open a contract through a private link and a one-time code. They don’t need an account, and nobody else can open it.

Clear policies

Read exactly what we collect and why in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Questions? Email us.
