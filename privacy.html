Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Rotely.ai (“Rotely,” “we,” “us”) collects when you use our website and app, how we use it, and the choices you have. Rotely is in private beta, and we’ll update this policy as the product changes.

What we collect

  • Waitlist and account details. Your name, email address, and the plan you tell us you’re interested in.
  • Your documents. Contracts and templates you upload, drafts you create, redlines, comments, and signed copies.
  • Signer and recipient details. Names and email addresses of the people you share a contract with or send it to for signature.
  • Signing records. Timestamps and related details that show who signed a document and when.
  • Identity verification photos. If you turn on ID verification, signers provide a photo of their ID and a selfie.
  • Basic usage information. Information our systems need to run the service, such as device and browser type and log data.

How we use it

  • To provide Rotely: drafting, sharing, redlining, signing, and tracking your contracts.
  • To run AI features you ask for, such as drafting from notes, suggesting counters, and summarizing redlines.
  • To confirm a signer’s identity when ID verification is on. ID and selfie photos are used only for that purpose and are kept with that contract’s signing record.
  • To contact you about the waitlist, your account, and important changes to the service.
  • To keep Rotely secure and to fix problems.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t use your documents to train any AI model.
  • We don’t show your documents to other Rotely users. Only you and the people you share a document with can see it.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run Rotely, and only as needed for them to do that work. This includes providers that host the app and website, collect waitlist sign-ups, power AI features, and process electronic signatures. E-signatures are powered by DocuSeal. We may also share information if the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of Rotely and its users.

How long we keep it

We keep your documents and account information for as long as your account is active, or as long as needed to provide the service. Signed contracts and their signing records are kept so they remain available as evidence of the signature. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time, and we’ll do so unless we’re required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to be removed from the waitlist, by emailing closeit@rotely.ai. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we’ll honor them.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting access to your documents to you and the people you share them with, and sharing contracts through private links with one-time codes. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us right away if you think your account has been compromised.

Children

Rotely is a business tool and isn’t meant for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we’ll update the date at the top and let account holders know by email.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email closeit@rotely.ai.
